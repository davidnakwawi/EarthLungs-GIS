Telluverd Framework

Eight connected intelligence modules

Select sites and run spatial analyses through the Telluverd analytical environment.

01

ATLAS

Site & Boundary Intelligence

Site boundaries, physical site structure, country organization and spatial site selection.

02

CANOPY

Vegetation Intelligence

NDVI, NDRE, EVI, vegetation comparisons and vegetation change analysis.

03

TERRA

Land & Water Intelligence

Land-cover mapping, NDWI, land-cover change and water-related analysis.

04

RELIEF

Terrain Intelligence

Elevation, slope, aspect, hillshade, contours and terrain statistics.

05

HYDRA

Hydrology Intelligence

Drainage, water movement, wetness, hydrological suitability and catchment analysis.

06

COAST

Coastal & Mangrove Intelligence

Mangrove extent, coastal change, inundation, intertidal conditions and connectivity.

07

RESTORE

Restoration Intelligence

Restoration suitability, plantable areas, trenchable areas and restoration monitoring.

08

AERIAL

Drone Intelligence

UAV orthomosaics, multispectral vegetation indices, terrain products and drone comparisons.
