GIS ARCHITECTURE

Site boundaries, physical site structure, country organization and spatial site selection.

02

CANOPY

Vegetation Intelligence

NDVI, NDRE, EVI, vegetation comparisons and vegetation change analysis.

03

Land-cover mapping, NDWI, land-cover change and water-related analysis.

04

Elevation, slope, aspect, hillshade, contours and terrain statistics.

05

08

UAV orthomosaics, multispectral vegetation indices, terrain products and drone comparisons.

STANDARD WORKFLOW

From data to restoration decisions

EarthLungs GIS follows a structured spatial workflow designed to keep environmental data traceable, reproducible and useful for restoration decisions.

01

Understand

Identify available spatial, field and remote sensing data.

02

Standardize

Organize site boundaries and datasets into consistent structures.

03

Analyse

04

Monitor

Compare environmental conditions through time and across restoration sites.

05

Decide

Convert spatial evidence into practical restoration intelligence.

RESTORATION INTELLIGENCE

Built for real restoration landscapes

EarthLungs GIS is designed around the practical information required to understand restoration sites: vegetation condition, land cover, terrain, hydrology, coastal processes, suitability and field-scale drone observations.

The objective is not simply to produce maps. The objective is to produce spatial evidence that supports restoration planning, monitoring and decision-making.

Satellite Sentinel-2, Dynamic World and other Earth observation datasets
GIS Spatial analysis, boundaries, terrain, hydrology and suitability modelling
Drone High-resolution multispectral and terrain intelligence from UAV surveys
