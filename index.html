RESTORATION CONTEXT

Built for real restoration landscapes.

EarthLungs GIS is designed around the practical information required to understand restoration sites: vegetation condition, land cover, terrain, hydrology, coastal processes, suitability and field-scale drone observations.

The objective is not simply to produce maps. It is to produce spatial evidence that supports restoration planning, monitoring and decision-making.

SatelliteSentinel-2, Dynamic World and other Earth observation datasets.
GISSpatial analysis, boundaries, terrain, hydrology and suitability modelling.
DroneHigh-resolution multispectral and terrain intelligence from UAV surveys.
