Telluverd integrates spatial data, remote sensing, terrain information, and restoration-oriented analyses into a single operational platform.

Workflow

How the platform works

A structured spatial workflow designed to keep environmental data traceable, reproducible and useful for restoration decisions.

01

Select a site

Choose a country and restoration site from the Telluverd site network.

02

Choose an analysis

Select from the available analytical modules based on your assessment needs.

03

Set assessment dates

Specify the relevant date ranges where temporal analysis is supported.

04

Run the analysis

Execute the spatial analysis in the live GIS application and view results on the map.

05

Interpret results

Use the generated maps, legends and statistics within their environmental and operational context.

Geographic Coverage

Connected restoration landscapes

Telluverd currently organizes spatial information across three countries in East and Southern Africa.

Site network across Kenya, Tanzania and Mozambique

Kenya

Mangrove and restoration landscapes, including coastal and inland project sites.

Tanzania

Coastal restoration sites and landscape intelligence across Tanzania.

Mozambique

Spatial datasets supporting regional restoration planning and monitoring.

Capabilities & Applications

Built for real restoration landscapes

Telluverd is designed around the practical information required to understand restoration sites.

Environmental monitoring

Track environmental conditions through time using satellite and drone observations.

Vegetation assessment

Analyse vegetation health and change using spectral indices such as NDVI, NDRE and EVI.

Land-cover analysis

Map land use and land cover, including change detection between assessment periods.

Restoration opportunity screening

Identify potentially suitable restoration and plantable areas using spatial criteria.

Site-level planning

Support site-level restoration planning with terrain, hydrology and suitability context.

Coastal and mangrove assessment

Examine mangrove extent, coastal change and intertidal conditions where applicable.

Drone-based mapping

Integrate high-resolution UAV data for detailed site-scale spatial evidence.

Spatial decision support

Convert spatial evidence into structured inputs for restoration decisions.

Remote-sensing screening results provide spatial evidence to guide restoration planning. They do not replace field validation. A remote-sensing screening result is not automatically a field-verified finding.
