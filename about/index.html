About Telluverd

Spatial intelligence for ecological restoration

Telluverd is a modular spatial intelligence platform designed to organize, analyse and communicate environmental information supporting ecological restoration across Africa.

Purpose

Turning environmental data into useful decisions

Restoration landscapes generate large amounts of spatial information. Site boundaries, satellite imagery, terrain, hydrology, vegetation, coastal conditions and drone surveys all provide different pieces of evidence.

Telluverd brings these datasets together into a structured analytical framework so that spatial information can support restoration planning, monitoring and decision-making.

The platform is designed around real restoration sites rather than maps alone. The objective is not simply to produce maps. The objective is to produce spatial evidence that supports restoration planning, monitoring and decision-making.

Approach

Satellite and drone-based spatial analysis

The relationship between spatial evidence and field validation

Remote-sensing screening results provide spatial evidence to guide restoration planning. They do not replace field validation. A remote-sensing screening result is not automatically a field-verified finding. Spatial analysis supports decision-making by identifying where field verification and restoration action are most warranted.

Satellite remote sensing

Earth observation data supports vegetation, land-cover, water and environmental change analysis across sites.

Drone-based mapping

UAV data provides high-resolution site-scale multispectral and spatial information for detailed assessment.

Eight Connected GIS Modules

The module framework

Each module has a defined analytical role while remaining part of the wider restoration information system.

01

ATLAS

Site & Boundary Intelligence

Site boundaries, physical site structure, country organization and spatial site selection.

02

CANOPY

Vegetation Intelligence

NDVI, NDRE, EVI, vegetation comparisons and vegetation change analysis.

03

TERRA

Land & Water Intelligence

Land-cover mapping, NDWI, land-cover change and water-related analysis.

04

RELIEF

Terrain Intelligence

Elevation, slope, aspect, hillshade, contours and terrain statistics.

05

HYDRA

Hydrology Intelligence

Drainage, water movement, wetness, hydrological suitability and catchment analysis.

06

COAST

Coastal & Mangrove Intelligence

Mangrove extent, coastal change, inundation, intertidal conditions and connectivity.

07

RESTORE

Restoration Intelligence

Restoration suitability, plantable areas, trenchable areas and restoration monitoring.

08

AERIAL

Drone Intelligence

UAV orthomosaics, multispectral vegetation indices, terrain products and drone comparisons.

Geographic Focus

A consistent regional spatial foundation

Telluverd currently organizes spatial information across three countries in East and Southern Africa.

Kenya

Mangrove and restoration landscapes, including coastal and inland project sites.

Tanzania

Coastal restoration sites and landscape intelligence across Tanzania.

Mozambique

Spatial datasets supporting regional restoration planning and monitoring.
