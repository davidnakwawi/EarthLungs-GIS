ABOUT EARTHLUNGS GIS

Spatial intelligence for ecological restoration

EarthLungs GIS is a modular spatial intelligence platform designed to organize, analyse and communicate environmental information supporting restoration across Africa.

OUR PURPOSE

Turning environmental data into useful decisions

Restoration landscapes generate large amounts of spatial information. Site boundaries, satellite imagery, terrain, hydrology, vegetation, coastal conditions and drone surveys all provide different pieces of evidence.

EarthLungs GIS brings these datasets together into a structured analytical framework so that spatial information can support restoration planning, monitoring and decision-making.

The platform is designed around real restoration sites rather than maps alone.

PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

Eight connected GIS modules

EarthLungs GIS is organized into eight specialist intelligence modules. Each module has a defined analytical role while remaining part of the wider restoration information system.

01

ATLAS

Site & Boundary Intelligence

Establishes the spatial foundation of the platform through site boundaries, country organization, physical site grouping and site selection.

02

CANOPY

Vegetation Intelligence

Provides vegetation analysis using NDVI, NDRE, EVI, NDVI and NDRE comparisons and vegetation change analysis.

03

TERRA

Land & Water Intelligence

Provides land-cover and water intelligence including LULC, NDWI, land-cover change and NDWI change analysis.

04

RELIEF

Terrain Intelligence

Analyses elevation and terrain conditions through elevation, slope, aspect, hillshade, contours and terrain statistics.

05

HYDRA

Hydrology Intelligence

Examines water movement and hydrological conditions including drainage, wetness, water connectivity and hydrological restoration suitability.

06

COAST

Coastal & Mangrove Intelligence

Supports coastal restoration analysis through mangrove extent, mangrove change, shoreline and water extent change, inundation, intertidal exposure and water connectivity.

07

RESTORE

Restoration Intelligence

Combines environmental factors to support restoration suitability, plantable area, trenchable area, restoration change and restoration monitoring.

08

AERIAL

Drone Intelligence

Brings high-resolution UAV information into the platform, including orthomosaics, multispectral vegetation indices, terrain products and drone-to-satellite comparison.
